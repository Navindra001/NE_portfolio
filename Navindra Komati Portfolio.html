About Me

Navindra KomatiAI engineer · London, UK

I am an AI and ML engineer who likes taking a model all the way to something people can use: the data, the model, the API and the interface. Most of my work is in healthcare and fintech, where accuracy and clear explanations matter.

I have completed a Master of Data Science at the University of Leicester, after a B.Tech in AI and Data Science. I have worked as an intern on generative AI at Google Cloud, machine learning at Stack AI Solutions, cloud on AWS and data science at APSSDC.

My most recent project is VAGUS AI, a platform for virtual-patient OSCE practice and for analysing healthcare opinion data. I am now looking for AI engineering, data science and applied AI research roles.
